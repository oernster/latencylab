Download LatencyLab

Simulate your architecture's latency before you build it: LatencyLab runs explicit models of your software architecture (tasks, events, queues, contention) thousands of times, to show where users would wait and why, before the code exists.

Pick your platform and you have the newest release. Free and GPL-3.0 + LGPL-3.0 with no cloud and no account; everything stays local on your machine.
